PAC Digital - Pokemon Auto Chess board game (prototype, personal use)
HOW TO PLAY: double-click index.html (works offline in any modern browser).
You = 1 human vs 7 bots with different personalities. 12 rounds, animated duels (speed/skip controls).
Tips: Hint button suggests a move; Auto lineup fills your lineup; shift-click buys quickly;
game autosaves (Continue on title screen). Settings & Rules are in the header.
Bot personalities are in src/bots.js (PERSONAS). Engine is a port of the Python sim.
